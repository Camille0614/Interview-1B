Three words to describe your style?

Feminine, luxury, and expressive.

Feminine

Tina likes pink and white, fitted clothing, silk, cotton, and dresses. She wants her style to feel elegant and feminine.

Luxury

Tina prefers high end and mature fashion. Her favorite piece is a Zimmermann dress that she bought in France. She also pays close attention to fabric, cut, and details.

Expressive

Tina sees clothing as a way to express herself. She said that she is not always good at expressing herself through talking, so clothes make it easier to show people what she likes.
