Personal Style

How would you describe your personal style?

I think, like, more luxury? More high end. And maybe a little complicated. I don't really like things that are too plain. I like something that looks more mature and kind of dramatic.

Was your style always like that?

No, very different. In middle school I liked cute Korean style a lot. Like softer colors, cute skirts, that kind of thing. Then I got older and slowly started liking more mature things.

Why do you think it changed?

Probably the internet. When I started using social media more, I saw a lot of celebrities and influencers. I like people like Dilraba and Wang Churan. Their styling is really pretty and polished.

Favorite Clothing

What's your favorite piece of clothing you own?

Probably my Zimmermann dress. I bought it in France, and that trip was really fun. So I have a good memory with it.

What do you usually wear on a normal day?

Honestly, very basic. Like T shirt, pants, comfortable things. I don't dress up every day.

Fashion and Personality

Do you think your clothes show your personality?

Yeah, I think so. I'm not super good at expressing myself when I talk. So I think clothes are kind of easier. I don't have to say anything, but people can still see what I like.

What do you want people to think when they see the way you dress?

Maybe that I have taste. I want people to know I care about details and how things look. I don't want it to feel random.

What feeling do you want to give?

Elegant, confident, and feminine. But not too boring.

Social Media

Do trends influence you a lot?

Yeah. I'm online a lot, so obviously yes. I look at outfits online all the time.

Does social media make your style better or make you buy more things?

Both, honestly. It gives me ideas, which is good. But sometimes I see too many things and feel like I need something new.

Future Style

Has Parsons changed your style?

Not really yet because I'm only a freshman. But a lot of people there have really strong personal style.

How do you think your style will change in the future?

Maybe more mature and more refined. I think I'll still like luxury things and dresses, but maybe I'll choose things more carefully.

Three words to describe your style?

Feminine, luxury, and expressive.
