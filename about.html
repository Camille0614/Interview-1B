Who is Tina?

Tina is a freshman studying at Parsons. She is interested in fashion, design, and personal style.

Her Background

Tina's style has changed a lot over time. In middle school, she liked cute Korean style with softer colors and cute skirts. As she got older, she became more interested in mature, feminine, and dramatic fashion.

Her Interests

Tina likes luxury fashion, dresses, and clothing with strong details. She also gets inspiration from celebrities, influencers, and social media.

Fashion and Personality

For Tina, fashion is also a way to express her personality. She likes clothing that feels elegant, confident, feminine, and expressive.
